VCPeer Live vs Restream

Restream’s free tier covers 2 channels at 720p with a 6-hour recording cap and Restream’s own branding burned into the stream. Adding a 3rd destination means jumping to Standard ($16–19/mo); 1080p and Studio guests need Professional ($39–49/mo); team seats and embed are Business-only ($199–239/mo).

Restream's weakest point: destination count is paywalled from channel 3 onward, and the free tier carries Restream’s own watermark on every stream.

What Restream does well

To be fair: Restream's strongest feature is the largest destination catalog (30+ platforms) and a mature in-browser Studio for guests. VCPeer Live doesn't try to out-feature that — it wins on price (free, no destination cap) and on being pure RTMP passthrough with no re-encode step.
