VCPeer Live vs Switchboard Live

Switchboard Live has no free tier. Indie ($35/mo) buys 3+ destinations but only 25 hours of total streaming per month at 720p/2.5Mbps. Pro ($99/mo) raises that to 50 hours at 1080p with 5+ destinations. Business is $350/mo, and pricing has reportedly gone quote-only for some tiers.

Switchboard Live's weakest point: there is no free tier, no studio/guest features at all, and a monthly streaming-HOUR cap even on paid plans — $35/mo buys only 25 hours of total streaming time, unusable for anyone who streams daily.

What Switchboard Live does well

To be fair: Switchboard Live's strongest feature is truly unlimited destination count per plan and a GSA contract for government/public-sector buyers. VCPeer Live doesn't try to out-feature that — it wins on price (free, no destination cap) and on being pure RTMP passthrough with no re-encode step.
