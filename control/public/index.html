VCPeer Live

Free multistream to Twitch, Kick and YouTube at once, plus a watch page with chat at vcpeer.com. The restream alternative that stays free.

Stream to Twitch and Kick at the same time, free

VCPeer Live is a free multistream tool: connect once with OBS or straight from your browser, and we fan your stream out to every destination you add — Twitch, Kick, YouTube or any custom RTMP(S) endpoint — with no transcoding and no per-destination re-encode. It's a free restream alternative built for streamers who don't want a subscription just to go live in two places.

Every stream also gets a watch page with live chat at vcpeer.com, so you have a destination you fully own even when the platforms change their rules.
